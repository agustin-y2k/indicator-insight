# Naming Decision

## Decision — keep “Indicator Insight” for now
The product/workspace/repository can continue to use **Indicator Insight** during the thesis.

## Why
1. Continuity: existing material, identifiers (`II-###`) and project recognition already use the name.
2. The scientific contribution no longer depends on the brand: the formal thesis title is domain-neutral.
3. “Indicator” can still refer broadly to evidence/indicators observed by the learner, even though the first adapter is financial.
4. Renaming now would create churn without improving the research design.

## Caveat
A web check does not constitute trademark clearance. The phrase is also used generically by third parties and in unrelated content. If the system becomes a public/commercial product, perform a formal trademark/domain/app-store review before branding investment.

## Naming layers
- **Thesis:** “Diseño y evaluación de una plataforma adaptativa e interpretable para el entrenamiento de decisiones bajo incertidumbre”.
- **Product:** Indicator Insight.
- **Core module/code:** `decision_core` / “Adaptive Decision Core”.
- **First adapter:** `finance`.

This makes the architecture reusable without forcing a rebrand.

## Revisit trigger
Re-evaluate the product name only if at least one condition occurs:
- a second domain becomes a first-class product module;
- advisor/panel considers “Indicator Insight” misleadingly financial;
- a conflict appears in trademark/product discovery;
- commercialization is planned.

## Avoid candidate churn
Do not choose a replacement merely because it sounds scientific. Several intuitive alternatives are already used by existing products. A future rebrand should pass distinctiveness, pronunciation in Spanish/English, domain availability and trademark screening together.
